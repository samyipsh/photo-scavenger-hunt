# Hillclimb
1. Ask what to climb and the target score. Screens or flows: use `hillclimb-ui`. Code, architecture or docs: use `council` for the reviews.
2. Each round, reviewers score and list findings. Triage each one: accept with discernment, don't be afraid to pushback and reject with a reason, or clarify with the user.
3. Stop and report when the (1) target is met, or (2) when scores stall or drop consistently across multiple rounds, or (3) whenever you feel quality is getting worse then you may pause and propose a different approach.
4. Report scores by round, what changed, and what was rejected and why.
